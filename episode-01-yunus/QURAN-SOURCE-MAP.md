# Episode 01 — Qur'an Source Map

**Story:** Prophet Yunus (Jonah), peace be upon him  
**Primary audience:** ages 2–4  
**Status:** editorial working document; qualified review required

## Qur'anic anchors

- **Surah Al-Anbiya 21:87–88:** Yunus (Dhun-Nun) leaves in anger; he calls out in the darknesses with the well-known supplication; Allah answers him and saves him from distress.
- **Surah As-Saffat 37:139–148:** Yunus is among the messengers; the account mentions the ship, the casting of lots, his being swallowed by the fish, his remembrance/glorification, being cast onto the shore while ill, and the gourd plant; the account then mentions his being sent to a people.

## Facts to retain
- Yunus is a prophet and messenger.
- The Qur'an describes him being swallowed by the fish and calling upon Allah.
- The exact supplication in 21:87 must be preserved and checked word-for-word.
- Allah answered him and saved him from distress (21:88).
- The account in 37:139–148 includes additional events; do not omit context in a way that changes the story's meaning.

## Do not invent as Qur'anic facts
- The name or species details of the fish beyond the Qur'anic wording.
- Conversations inside the fish.
- How long Yunus remained inside.
- Exact physical details of the ship, storm, sea, shore or plant beyond what the verses establish.
- Emotional thoughts, dialogue or actions not established by the Qur'an.
- Any depiction of Prophet Yunus.

## Preschool explanation
Use simple narration such as: "Allah's prophet Yunus called upon Allah in the darkness. He praised Allah and asked for His help. Allah answered him and saved him from distress." A qualified reviewer must approve the final Arabic and translation.

## Fictional host boundary
Nour and Zuzu may introduce the episode before the story and ask a reflection question after it. They must not appear inside the Qur'anic events, meet Yunus, speak to him, or pretend to witness the events.
