## Scene 2 — Little fish (0:05.91–0:12.75)

Prompt: Wide 16:9 gentle storybook view of small colorful fish moving beneath clear turquoise water, sunlight patterns on the surface, calm shallow-water ambience. Keep Nour and Zuzu on the beach if included; they remain fictional hosts outside the sacred narrative. No text, no logos, no invented interaction with any prophet.

No visible mouth, no swallowing action, no storm, no ship, no people, no prophet depiction, no text, no logos. Keep the image neutral and contemplative.

The image is a symbolic visual metaphor only, not a literal reconstruction.

## Scene 5 — Allah answers and relief (0:41.06–0:47.56)

Prompt: Wide 16:9 peaceful open sea under soft returning daylight, warm light across gentle water, a calm horizon and hopeful pastel tones. Do not show a specific rescue action, shore landing, plant, or physical sequence. No people, no prophet depiction, no text, no logos.

## Scene 6 — Gentle takeaway (0:47.61–1:03.13)

Prompt: Wide 16:9 bright peaceful seaside garden, Nour the cream rabbit with teal scarf and Zuzu the golden-yellow bird facing the audience, friendly reassuring expressions, simple clean background, warm morning light, open lower third for subtitles, no text, no logos.

## Production guardrails

- Generate each scene separately, not as a collage or storyboard sheet.
- Keep Nour and Zuzu designs consistent in scenes 1, 2 and 6.
- Never depict Allah or Prophet Yunus. Do not imply the fictional hosts witnessed Qur'anic events.
- Do not show a whale swallowing anything, a mouth interior, or an invented sequence of events.
- Avoid specific depictions of ship, storm, lots, shore/landing, gourd plant or other details unless explicitly approved against the cited verses and intended script.
- Do not generate Quranic Arabic inside images; insert only verified text in editing.
- Keep movement calm; avoid flashes, sudden cuts, frightening expressions, or loud effects.
- Review every generated image for accidental religious depictions, extra limbs, distorted faces, and character consistency before use.
- The existing rendered MP4 still contains the previous whale-mouth scene and is NOT visually approved. Rebuild it from approved replacement scene assets after a human storyboard review.
