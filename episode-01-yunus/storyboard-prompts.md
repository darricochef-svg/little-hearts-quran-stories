# Episode 01 — Visual storyboard and generation prompts
Status: production brief only; not approved for publication.
Format: 16:9, 1920x1080, polished 3D storybook hybrid, gentle shapes, soft cinematic lighting, pastel ocean blues and warm golden highlights, calm pacing for ages 2–4.
Recurring fictional hosts: Nour, a small cream-colored rabbit with soft rounded features and a simple teal scarf; Zuzu, a tiny golden-yellow bird with a small orange beak. Keep character design consistent. They are fictional presenters, not characters from the Qur'anic account.

## Scene 1 — Welcome by the sea (0:00–0:15)
Prompt: Wide 16:9 children's 3D storybook illustration of a peaceful sunny seaside, gentle turquoise water, soft sand, distant rounded green hills, Nour the cream rabbit wearing a teal scarf standing beside Zuzu the tiny golden-yellow bird, both smiling softly, pastel palette, rounded toy-like forms, safe and calm, clear empty space in lower third for subtitles, no text, no logos.

## Scene 2 — Little fish (0:15–0:30)
Prompt: Wide 16:9 underwater 3D storybook scene with small colorful fish swimming through clear turquoise water, sunbeams and tiny bubbles, a hint of the shore above, Nour and Zuzu watching safely from the sandy beach in the background, gentle wonder, no text, no logos, subtitle-safe lower third.

## Scene 3 — Introducing the story (0:30–0:55)
Prompt: Wide 16:9 respectful children's storybook ocean scene, a large whale seen only as a gentle distant silhouette beneath the water surface, beautiful blue gradients, small fish for scale, calm rather than scary, no human figures, no prophet depiction, no text, no logos, subtitle-safe lower third.

## Scene 4 — Calling upon Allah (0:55–1:20)
Prompt: Wide 16:9 abstract, gentle underwater darkness with soft blue light rays, bubbles and the distant indistinct silhouette of a whale, peaceful and non-frightening, visual metaphor for being in darkness, no person or human silhouette, no writing or calligraphy, no depiction of Allah, no text, no logos, subtitle-safe lower third. Add the exact prayer only later as verified Arabic subtitles, not as generated image text.

## Scene 5 — Relief and rescue (1:20–1:45)
Prompt: Wide 16:9 luminous blue ocean as warm sunlight returns through the water, distant whale silhouette swimming peacefully away toward open sea, sparkling surface and soft golden rays, hopeful gentle mood, no human figures, no prophet depiction, no text, no logos, subtitle-safe lower third. Do not invent a specific rescue scene beyond the narration.

## Scene 6 — Gentle takeaway (1:45–2:15)
Prompt: Wide 16:9 bright peaceful seaside garden, Nour the cream rabbit with teal scarf and Zuzu the golden-yellow bird together facing the audience, friendly reassuring expressions, rounded 3D storybook design, warm golden morning light, simple clean background, open lower third for subtitles, no text, no logos.

## Production guardrails
- Generate each scene separately, not as a collage or storyboard sheet.
- Keep the two host designs consistent across scenes 1, 2 and 6.
- Never depict Allah. Do not depict Prophet Yunus or imply the fictional hosts witnessed Qur'anic events.
- Do not generate Quranic Arabic inside images; add verified subtitles in editing.
- Keep movement slow, avoid flashes, sudden cuts, frightening expressions, or loud effects.
- Review generated images for accidental religious depictions, extra limbs, distorted faces, and consistency before use.
