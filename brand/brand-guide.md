# Brand Guide — Little Hearts, Quran Stories
Arabic working title: حكايات قرآنية لقلوب صغيرة
Tagline: Little Stories. Beautiful Lessons.

## Palette
- Garden green: #E7F4ED
- Storybook gold: #F8E7B7
- Sky turquoise: #BFE8E6
- Soft coral: #F6CFC7

## Characters
- Nour (نور): gentle cream rabbit, curious and kind.
- Zuzu (زوزو): small golden-yellow bird, playful and encouraging.
They are fictional recurring hosts only, not Quranic characters and never portrayed as interacting with prophets as if part of revelation.

## Visual guardrails
- Do not depict Allah or visually depict any prophet.
- Soft storybook textures, rounded forms, gentle camera movement and calm pacing.
- Avoid flashing, harsh cuts, frightening imagery and over-stimulation.
- Keep one clear action per scene and maintain consistent character designs.
